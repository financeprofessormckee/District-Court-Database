# Changelog

## 0.3.1 (2026-10-31)

First public release.

- 44,275 records, 66 variables, in CSV and Stata formats, with a PDF codebook and a
  machine-readable dictionary.
- `appointing_president` distinguishes George H. W. Bush from George W. Bush (previously
  "George Bush" for both). 12,474 cells changed; no other values changed.
- Court lookup tables carry readable court names alongside the original codes.

Earlier versions were internal and not distributed.
